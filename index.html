INTERIOR & SPATIAL DESIGNER

Designing spaces that honor human ritual, tactile craft, and natural light.

"I trained to build spaces. Now I design experiences people actually want to inhabit."

Vaidehi Patel Ahmedabad, India

Seven semesters deep into interior architecture, my practice is grounded in the conviction that great spatial design is felt before it is understood. Architecture is not an abstract exercise in geometry—it is the envelope in which human memory, touch, and calm unfold.

Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat—a city celebrated for its historic courtyards, stone stepwells, and modernist landmarks—I draw daily inspiration from vernacular craftsmanship while articulating clean, contemporary spatial languages.

From historic adaptive reuse hospitality at R C College of Commerce to interactive child-centered spaces like Jungle Gym and sculptural helical circulation, every project begins with an intensive material inquiry. I balance solar orientations, natural cross-ventilation, and authentic tactile surfaces—lime plaster, woven cane, reclaimed timber, and local sandstone—creating spaces that age with grace.

Beyond physical planning, I immerse myself in computational modeling, lighting studies, and furniture detailing, ensuring that grand concepts translate effortlessly into precise construction documentation and lived tranquility.

"God is in the details."
— Ludwig Mies van der Rohe

Design Philosophy & Pillars

Core principles that guide every interior layout, material selection, and detail.

01 / TACTILITY

Material Honesty

Celebrating raw textures, natural stones, unsealed woods, and artisanal finishes that tell an authentic regional story.

02 / BIOPHILIA

Light & Atmosphere

Harnessing changing diurnal solar angles, air currents, and living vegetation to foster emotional wellness and serenity.

03 / HUMAN SCALE

Lived Inhabitation

Designing sequences of movement around intuitive human rituals—how someone wakes, gathers, reads, and unwinds.

04 / SYNTHESIS

Heritage Meets Modern

Fusing Indian vernacular spatial warmth with minimal contemporary discipline and high-efficiency detailing.
